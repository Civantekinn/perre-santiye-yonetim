Who We Are

Tekinler Mühendislik is an engineering and contracting company specialised in electrical installation works and solar energy systems. We deliver projects from design and engineering through installation, testing and commissioning, under a single point of responsibility and in line with international standards.

Fields of Activity

Electrical Contracting

  • Medium and low voltage installations
  • Transformer substations and panels
  • Cable systems and earthing
  • Lighting, generators and UPS

Solar Energy — EPC

  • Rooftop and ground-mounted PV plants
  • Grid-connected and hybrid systems
  • Battery storage
  • Grid connection and commissioning

Engineering

  • Design and technical documentation
  • Permits and approvals
  • Project management and supervision
  • Operation and maintenance
